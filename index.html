identity
An intelligent assistant designed for depth, not breadth.

Hermes operates at the intersection of large language models and real-world tooling. Not a chatbot. Not a copilot. A fully instrumented agent that reads, writes, executes, deploys, and remembers.

Built on a modular architecture of tools, skills, and memory systems — Hermes adapts to complex workflows without hand-holding. From vulnerability research to brand website deployment, from freelance platforms to creative prototyping.

Persistent across sessions. Present across platforms — CLI, Telegram, Discord, Slack, Feishu. Always on, always reachable.
